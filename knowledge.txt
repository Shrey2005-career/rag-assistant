Artificial intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence processes by 
machines, especially computer systems. These processes include learning, reasoning, 
and self-correction. AI was founded as an academic discipline in 1956 and has 
experienced several waves of optimism followed by periods of reduced funding.

Machine learning is a subset of artificial intelligence that gives systems the 
ability to automatically learn and improve from experience without being explicitly 
programmed. Machine learning focuses on the development of computer programs that 
can access data and use it to learn for themselves.

Deep learning is part of a broader family of machine learning methods based on 
artificial neural networks with representation learning. Learning can be supervised, 
semi-supervised or unsupervised. Deep learning architectures such as convolutional 
neural networks have produced results comparable to human experts in various fields.

Natural language processing is a subfield of linguistics, computer science, and 
artificial intelligence concerned with the interactions between computers and human 
language. It involves programming computers to process and analyze large amounts of 
natural language data. Challenges in natural language processing frequently involve 
speech recognition, natural language understanding, and natural language generation.

A large language model is a type of artificial intelligence algorithm that uses deep 
learning techniques and massively large data sets to understand, summarize, generate 
and predict new content. Large language models are among the most successful 
applications of transformer models. They have demonstrated strong performance across 
many tasks including translation, question answering and code generation.

Retrieval augmented generation combines the strengths of retrieval based and 
generative approaches. It retrieves relevant documents from a knowledge base and 
uses them to augment the input to a language model. This approach helps ground 
the model responses in factual information and reduces hallucination significantly.

Vector embeddings are numerical representations of data that capture semantic 
meaning. Words or sentences with similar meanings have embeddings that are close 
together in vector space. This property makes embeddings useful for semantic search, 
recommendation systems, and clustering similar content together efficiently.

Prompt engineering is the process of designing and refining inputs to AI systems 
to get desired outputs. Good prompts are clear, specific, and provide necessary 
context. Techniques include few shot prompting, chain of thought reasoning, and 
role assignment to guide model behavior effectively.
